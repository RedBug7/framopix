## What is Framopix?

Framopix is a fast, lightweight desktop utility built specifically for photographers, videographers and studio owners. It handles the repetitive, time-consuming parts of your post-shoot workflow — so you can focus on your creativity.

No subscriptions. No cloud. No bloat. Just install and run.

---

## Features

### 🗂️ Match & Copy
Automatically match your RAW or edited files from a shortlisted folder to your main data folder — by filename. Copies matched files instantly to your output folder.

Perfect for wedding and event photographers who shortlist JPEGs but need to deliver RAWs.

### 🗜️ Compress Images
Batch compress JPG, PNG and WEBP images with 4 quality presets:

| Preset | Quality | Best for |
|--------|---------|---------|
| High Quality | 90% | Client delivery |
| Balanced | 70% | Web upload |
| Aggressive | 50% | Storage saving |
| Heavy | 30% | Thumbnails |

### ✨ Auto Image Enhance
One-click batch enhancement with 3 presets — Basic, Natural and Aggressive.

| Preset | White Balance | CLAHE | Face Softening | Sharpness | Best for |
|--------|-------------|-------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| Basic | Light | Low | Minimal | None | Quick edits |
| Natural | Moderate | Medium | Subtle | Slight | Everyday delivery |
| Aggressive | Strong | High | Natural | Enhanced | Portfolio work |
